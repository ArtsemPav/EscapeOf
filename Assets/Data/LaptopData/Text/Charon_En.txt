"A mortal seeks passage with his feet, but the boundary is crossed by sight.

The gates between life and eternal rest are deaf to iron and keys. They demand only one thing — to close the eyes of the one who remains to guard the threshold.

Place an obol in the empty eye sockets of the skull. This is not a bribe, but payment to the Ferryman for the right to go blind to the world of the living and to see clearly on the other side.

Do not stint on the second obol — one-eyed shadows wander the shore forever."
